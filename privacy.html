Privacy Policy for GanttManagementSheet

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

GanttManagementSheet is a Google Sheets-based project and task management solution designed to help users manage schedules, gantt charts, kanban boards, workload planning, project tracking, and reporting workflows.

This Privacy Policy explains how GanttManagementSheet accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when users interact with the application and related Google services.

Information We Access

Depending on the features used, GanttManagementSheet may access:

  • Spreadsheet data within Google Sheets used by the user for project management, scheduling, task tracking, reporting, gantt charts, kanban boards, and workload management.
  • Basic Google account information such as the active user's email address when required for authentication, licensing, subscription validation, or identifying the current user.
  • Metadata necessary to operate application features, automation, menus, reports, and integrations.

How We Use Google User Data

Google user data is used solely for providing and improving the functionality of GanttManagementSheet, including:

  • Scheduling tasks and project timelines
  • Generating gantt charts and workload views
  • Managing project and kanban data
  • Creating reports and performance summaries
  • Supporting automation and spreadsheet workflows
  • Subscription or licensing validation
  • Providing support and troubleshooting

GanttManagementSheet does not use Google user data for advertising purposes, profiling, or marketing personalization.

Google API Scopes

GanttManagementSheet requests only the Google API permissions necessary for application functionality and follows the principle of least privilege.

Requested permissions may include access to:

  • Google Sheets spreadsheets and spreadsheet data
  • Google Apps Script services required by the application
  • Basic account information for authentication purposes

Data Sharing

GanttManagementSheet does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties.

Data may only be disclosed:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the security or integrity of the service
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, or technical issues
  • When explicitly authorized by the user for a requested feature

Spreadsheet data is primarily processed within Google services. GanttManagementSheet does not transfer spreadsheet contents to external databases controlled by the developer except where explicitly required for application functionality.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users maintain control over their spreadsheet data stored within their Google account.

Users may delete their data at any time by:

  • Deleting spreadsheet files
  • Removing the Apps Script project
  • Revoking Google account permissions
  • Uninstalling or discontinuing use of the application

Limited operational records such as licensing or support-related information may be temporarily retained for legitimate business and security purposes.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to help protect user information and maintain the security of the application.

GanttManagementSheet follows the principle of least privilege and requests only permissions necessary for intended functionality.

Third-Party Services

GanttManagementSheet operates using Google services including Google Sheets and Google Apps Script. Use of those services is also subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Google API Services User Data Policy

GanttManagementSheet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, feature updates, legal requirements, or operational changes.

Continued use of GanttManagementSheet after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
